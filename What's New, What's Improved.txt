DAH: Re-Enlightened Version 1.1

Fixes, Improvements, and Tweaks

- Improved weapon damage for the zap-o-matic, disintegrator ray, anal probe, and death ray
- Tweaked health values for enemy vehicles like the attack helicopters, tanks, SAM trucks, and Nexo saucers
- Rocket attacks from the helicopters are more easily dodgeable both in the saucer and on foot
- Decreased how often helicopters launch their rockets at you so that it isn't a constant barrage
- Lowered the DNA values for abducting humans from an exploitable 25 per human to a more reasonable 10 per human
- Increased DNA payout from beating missions, side missions, and challenges
- Soldiers no longer spawn in the deserts and hills during military alert, only on final alert
- Decreased how often Crypto taunts, so he's less of a chatterbox while destroying humans
- Improved Crypto's head turning so he isn't bobbing his head left and right at the sight of humans
- Fixed a small issue where Crypto was visible during a conversation in Invasion of the Bodymorphers
- Replaced the "star bomb" explosion used during Hex on the Beach with the proper Nexo arrival particle FX
(It's used for the Nexo's that spawn when you're doing the lock puzzle on the human cage)
- Increased the time between respawns for the humans used in Human Darts
- Added explosion FX to the Space Dust signs

How to Install

- Uninstall your current mod by clicking the "Uninstall" button in TFC Installer. 
- Install the new build
- Play, have fun!

DO NOT INSTALL THE NEW VERSION OF THE MOD OVER THE CURRENT VERSION. THIS CAUSES ERRORS.
