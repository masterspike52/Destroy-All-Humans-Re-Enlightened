How to Install Destroy All Humans: Re-Enlightened

Extracting the ISO (the legal way)

- Plug your thumb drive into the Xbox 360

- Put your Path of the Furon disc into the disc tray

- Install to thumb drive

- Take thumb drive to computer

- Convert file into ISO using the God2ISO tool https://github.com/raburton/god2iso

- Extract files using Goopie Launcher

Modifying the executable

- Run ConvertDefaultXex.exe

- select your default.xex and in a few seconds your executable is now mod friendly

Installing the Mod

- Open TFC Installer https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/588 (requires .net framework/runtime 6.0)

- Click Mod Folder, navigate to the "MyMod" folder located inside the "Re-Enlightened" folder

- Click Game Folder, navigate to where your POTF folder is, click on it

- Click "Update DAH: Path of the Furon" to re-enlighten your DAH experience

(Optional) Adding the Movies

- Unzip the folder labeled "Movies"

- Inside you will see two folder labeled "Menu Videos" and "Cutscenes"

- The first one contains an assortment of new main menu videos created by Destroy All Humans
Tribute and Sketso

- Pick whichever ones you want (classic, outer space, or lava lamp) and copy them
To your "Movies" folder inside the POTF folder

- Open "Cutscenes" to find all the remastered cutscenes created by Destroy All Humans Tribute

- One is labeled "Las Paradiso" and the other is labeled "Shen Long"

- Copy the FMV's in both folders and paste them inside your "Movies" folder inside the POTF folder

(Optional) Adding Audio Files

- Unzip the folder labeled "Audio"

- This contains all the higher quality audio files from the PS3 version that are compatible with
The 360 version

- Copy and paste those into your "Audio" folder inside your POTF folder

(Optional) Bloom patches

- There is a zipped folder labeled "Post Processing." This contains two patches for the
In-game post processing: "Native" and "High-Res"

- The native bloom values are there by default. "Native" is just a backup folder.

- "High-Res" is for those playing POTF at a higher resolution on Xenia Canary. It ensures
You have bloom without the artifacting that occurs because of the higher resolution.

- Copy and paste the included package patch assets into the "CookedXenon" folder located inside
Your "MyMod" folder. Click yes to overwrite.

- Again, Native is just a back-up folder in case you change your mind and want to play POTF at its 
Default resolution.

Adding the new Splash Screen

-Unzip the folder labeled "Splash." Inside is the new splash screen created by Destroy All Humans
Tribute

- Copy "splash.bmp" to where the vanilla "splash.bmp" is inside your POTF folder (Click on Splash,
Then click on Xbox 360 inside that folder to find the vanilla "splash.bmp")

Commonly Asked Questions

- Is Re-Enlightened compatible with ReDAHM?
Yes.

- (Xenia Only ) Why is the game stuck on the loading screen after the company credits?
You can fix this with the following input:

- Press down twice
- Press A
- Press down once
- Press A again

If done correctly, it'll take you to the extras menu.

- Are the outfits unlocked?
On ReDAHM yes, but not on Xenia Canary.
