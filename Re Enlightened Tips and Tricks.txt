Re-Enlightened Tips and Tricks

On-Foot Combat

- Need to draw the heat away from Crypto? Plant a Venus Human Trap! A mean green mother from outer space will definitely keep them busy.

- Feeling overwhelmed? Use temporal fist to make a quick escape, or to set ‘em up and knock ‘em down.

- The Venus Human Trap can be summoned anytime and anywhere while it’s active, so don’t just leave it all by its lonesome self!

- Short on ammo? The zap-o-matic is a great fallback weapon until you can transmog something for ammo.

Aerial Combat

- The Tornadotron is useful for clearing the skies and streets of enemies. Best of all, you can fire it while cloaked!

- The Quantum Deconstructor’s max upgrades give you ultimate power at the cost of extra ammo. Use wisely!

- The death ray makes quick work of ground units and smaller buildings, so don’t let it go to waste!

- The saucer’s cloak temporarily deactivates if Crypto is under attack, so give yourself some distance before trying to activate it.

DNA

- Thanks to Pox’s technical expertise, all of Crypto’s weapons now automatically eject brainstems from their victims. However, using certain weapons and abilities gives extra DNA , namely the anal probe, the extract ability, and the Venus Human Trap.

- A crowd of fleeing humans is easy prey for the saucer’s abduction beam. Suck ‘em up!

- Challenges can be replayed to better your score and earn extra DNA.

The Meditation Chamber

- Be sure to visit the mental chamber often to see if Crypto’s powers can be upgraded. The more you use them, the more enlightened (and stronger!) Crypto’s powers get.

Bosses

- Those bombs Meningitis’ bust spits out can be pretty annoying. However, they can be used against him! If you grab one while Temporal Fist is active, fling it back at the highlighted tube to deal extra damage. They can also be transmogged for ammo!

- Time Temporal Fist just right and the giant squid’s tentacles won’t be so damaging.

The Saucer

- Hold the left bumper to activate the saucer’s vertical mode, allowing you to change altitude instantly!

- Hold the X button (square on a PS controller) to activate the saucer’s horn and knock those silly humans aside!

Arkvoodle

- “If you suck five, you suck plenty.” No, that’s not a euphemism, it’s a reference to a certain gun that fires a certain vortex. A black hole, if you will.

Game Breaking Bugs

- The black hole gun is like Russian roulette. Its sheer power may or or may not crash the game, so be sure Crypto is at a safe distance before firing.

- DO NOT TAKE A HOOKER OUTSIDE THE KOWDUNG DISTRICT IN SHEN LONG OR A SOLDIER OUTSIDE THE MILITARY BASE IN LAS PARADISO. DOING THIS WILL NOT ONLY EJECT CRYPTO BUT
DISABLE ALL OF HIS MENTAL POWERS. YOU DO NOT WANT TO SCREW YOURSELF OVER.
